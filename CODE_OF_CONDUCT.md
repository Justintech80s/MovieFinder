# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be welcoming, constructive, and useful to contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and specific in technical discussion
- giving actionable feedback
- focusing criticism on code, design, and evidence rather than people
- helping newcomers understand the project
- documenting tradeoffs and decisions clearly

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project collaboration spaces.

## Reporting

If conduct in a project space becomes unsafe or abusive, use GitHub's reporting and moderation tools and avoid escalating the situation publicly.

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect productive collaboration.
